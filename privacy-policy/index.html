Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bodge Job Apps handles information when you use apps and services published under the Bodge Job Apps developer name.

Information we may collect

The information processed by an app depends on the features that app provides. This may include information you choose to provide, app activity, device or technical information, and content required for a feature to work.

Where an app requests access to device features such as the camera, photos, storage, notifications or location, access is used only where required for the relevant app feature and subject to the permissions you grant on your device.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide and operate app features, maintain security and reliability, diagnose technical problems, improve app performance, and support users.

Third-party services

Some Bodge Job Apps may use third-party service providers for functions such as hosting, authentication, data storage, analytics or other technical services. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and are governed by their own privacy terms.

Sharing of information

Bodge Job Apps does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate an app, where required by law, or where necessary to protect users, the service or the rights of others.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide an app feature, to meet legal obligations, or to maintain security and service integrity. Retention periods may vary depending on the app and the type of information involved.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of electronic storage or transmission can, however, be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

Bodge Job Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children except where an app is specifically designed to support family use and the collection is necessary for that feature. Where applicable, appropriate permissions and safeguards are used.

Your choices and rights

You can control many permissions through your device settings. Depending on your location and the information involved, you may also have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when apps, features, service providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the developer contact details shown on the relevant Bodge Job Apps listing in Google Play.
