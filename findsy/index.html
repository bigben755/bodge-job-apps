Family multiplayer game

Findsy

Take a photo. Hide something in it. Give everyone the first letter. First to find it wins.

A modern multiplayer spin on the classic idea of I Spy — built around your own photos, your own surroundings and the people you’re playing with.

Multiplayer Photo challenges Live guesses Family friendly

How to play

Four steps. One hidden Findsy.

There are no pre-made levels. Every round is created by the people playing, so the game can be completely different every time.

01

Take a photo

The main player photographs a scene with plenty for the other players to look through.

02

Hide your Findsy

Choose one object in the photo and enter what it is. Everyone else only gets its first letter.

03

Race to guess

The photo goes live. Players inspect it and send guesses while the countdown keeps running.

04

Winner takes the turn

The first correct player finds it, wins the round and becomes the main player for the next challenge.

Why Findsy works

The game board is whatever’s around you.

A living room, a garden, a holiday apartment or somewhere completely new. If you can photograph it, you can turn it into a challenge.

📸

Player-made challenges

No fixed question bank and no repeated levels. Every photo is a fresh puzzle made by somebody in the group.

⚡

Fast live rounds

Guesses arrive in real time while everyone races the clock and each other.

👑

The winner goes next

Find the hidden object first and the crown passes to you to create the next round.

Privacy & information

Photos are game content, not a permanent archive.

Findsy is designed to use the minimum data required for its multiplayer features. Ordinary challenge photos are temporary and are removed when they are no longer needed for the game.
