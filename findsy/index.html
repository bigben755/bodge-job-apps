Findsy

A modern multiplayer take on the classic game of I Spy — using your own photos.

Hide something in a photo, tell everyone what letter it begins with, then see who can Findsy it first.

How to play

Take it. Hide it. Findsy it.

Findsy turns the world around you into the game board. Play together on separate phones and race against friends and family to spot the hidden object.

1. Take a photo

The main player takes a photo containing an object for everyone else to find.

2. Choose your Findsy

Pick the hidden object, enter what it is and reveal only the first letter to the other players.

3. Start the hunt

Everyone studies the photo and submits their guesses while the clock is ticking.

4. Find it first

The first player to correctly identify the Findsy wins the round and becomes the main player for the next one.

Made for playing together

Every photo creates a new game

A telescope in the corner. A mug on a shelf. A tiny toy hiding in the background. Almost anything can become a Findsy.

Make your object obvious for younger players, hide it somewhere fiendishly difficult for the adults, or challenge everyone with something they walk past every day.

There are no fixed levels or pre-made pictures — the people playing create the challenges themselves.

Findsy features

Simple rules. Surprisingly competitive.

  • Multiplayer games across separate phones
  • Player-created photo challenges
  • Live guesses during each round
  • Timed hunts
  • Only the first letter is revealed
  • First correct answer wins the round
  • Winner takes the next turn
  • Family-friendly gameplay
  • No chat required
  • A different challenge every time you play

Example round

Beginning with T...

One player photographs a room containing a telescope.

Everyone else sees the photo and the clue “Beginning with T”.

Table? Television? Toaster?

The first player to guess Telescope finds it, wins the round and gets to create the next Findsy.

Privacy & information

Your photos belong in the game — not hanging around forever

Findsy uses the minimum data needed to operate its multiplayer features.

Photos submitted during ordinary games are temporary game content and are removed when they are no longer required.

You can find full details about the information Findsy uses, why it is needed and how long it is retained in our Privacy Policy.
