Findsy

Take it. Hide it. Findsy it.

One player takes a photo and chooses an object hidden somewhere inside it. Everyone else gets the first letter and races to work out what the Findsy is before the clock runs out.

The first correct player wins the round and creates the next challenge.

In development

SectionIQ

See the section. Understand the performance.

SectionIQ is a rail performance insight app designed to turn a user-started journey recording into useful operational evidence without demanding constant interaction from the person carrying the device.

It is being built to map journeys, measure station dwell and sectional running times, calculate average speeds and compare observed running against timetable information so recurring performance patterns are easier to see.

Prototype stage · Currently in development

About us

Apps without the boardroom.

Bodge Job Apps is a small independent developer building straightforward apps around ideas we actually want to use ourselves.

We’re not trying to build the next enormous social network, invent a new form of subscription, or add seventeen features nobody asked for. The goal is simpler: make something enjoyable or useful, make it easy to understand and make sure it works.

Some ideas are games. Some solve practical problems. Either way, we build, test, discover what we’ve accidentally broken, fix it, then keep improving from real feedback.

The Bodge Job method

  1. 01Have an idea.Usually at an inconvenient time.
  2. 02Build it.Try not to break anything important.
  3. 03Test it.Discover the things we definitely broke.
  4. 04Fix it.Properly. Mostly.
  5. 05Release it.Surprisingly functional.
“

Our philosophy

Does this really need to be complicated?

Usually, no. We like apps that make sense when you open them. Simple idea. Clear purpose. Whether it’s useful or just good fun, it should do its job without getting in the way.
