About us

Apps without the corporate nonsense.

Bodge Job Apps is a small independent app developer creating straightforward apps around ideas we genuinely think would be fun to use.

We're not trying to build the next enormous social network or cram an app full of features nobody asked for.

The idea is much simpler: spot something that could make life a little more entertaining, build it, test it, inevitably bodge something along the way, fix it, and put it out into the world.

Our apps are particularly inspired by games, challenges and experiences that friends and families can enjoy together.

THE BODGE JOB METHOD

  1. Have an idea. Usually at an inconvenient time.
  2. Build it. Try not to break anything important.
  3. Test it. Discover several things we definitely broke.
  4. Fix it. Mostly.
  5. Release it. Surprisingly functional.

Photo challenge game

Findsy

See it. Find it. Snap it.

Findsy turns the world around you into a photo challenge. One player chooses something to find, everyone else heads off looking for it, and the race is on.

Designed for families and friends, Findsy makes walks, holidays, days out and even boring afternoons a little more competitive.

Word game

Just Three Words

Three words. That's all you've got.

Just Three Words is a simple word game built around one restriction: whatever you're trying to say, describe or explain, you've only got three words to do it.

Quick to understand, easy to play and considerably harder than it sounds.
