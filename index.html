Just Three Words

Three words. That’s all you’ve got.

Just Three Words is a quick-fire game of clues, guesses and very limited vocabulary. Describe the answer, explain the idea or get your team onto the right track — but every clue has to fit into just three words.

The rules take seconds to learn. Finding three genuinely useful words is the difficult bit. Perfect for families, friends, parties and those moments when someone says, “What shall we do?”

About us

Apps without the boardroom.

Bodge Job Apps is a small independent developer building straightforward apps around ideas we actually want to use ourselves.

We’re not trying to build the next enormous social network, invent a new form of subscription, or add seventeen features nobody asked for. The goal is simpler: make something enjoyable, make it easy to understand and make sure it works.

Most of our ideas start with games, challenges and little experiences that are better when other people are involved. We build, test, discover what we’ve accidentally broken, fix it, then keep improving from real feedback.

The Bodge Job method

  1. 01Have an idea.Usually at an inconvenient time.
  2. 02Build it.Try not to break anything important.
  3. 03Test it.Discover the things we definitely broke.
