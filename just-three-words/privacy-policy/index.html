Just Three Words Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bodge Job Apps handles information when you use the Just Three Words mobile application.

Just Three Words is designed to be a simple game. The current version does not require a Bodge Job Apps account and is not intended to collect personal information from players.

1. Information you enter into the app

During play, you may enter words or short pieces of game text. This information is used to provide the game experience. Bodge Job Apps does not use gameplay text to build advertising profiles or sell it to third parties.

2. Personal information

Just Three Words does not ask you to create a Bodge Job Apps account and does not intentionally request information such as your name, home address, phone number, precise location or date of birth.

3. Advertising and analytics

The current version of Just Three Words does not use third-party advertising or behavioural advertising. Bodge Job Apps does not sell personal information.

4. Device permissions

Just Three Words should only request device permissions that are necessary for features provided by the app. If a future version introduces a feature requiring an additional permission, this policy and the app’s store disclosures will be updated where required.

5. Data retention

Any temporary gameplay information stored by the app is kept only for as long as needed to provide the relevant game functionality, unless the information is deliberately saved on your device as part of a feature you choose to use.

6. Children and families

Just Three Words is intended to be suitable for general and family use. We do not knowingly use the app to collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of connected devices and app-store accounts as appropriate.

7. Third-party services and app stores

Your device, operating system and app-store provider may process technical information independently when you download, install, update or use an app. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms, not this Privacy Policy.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to design the app so that it collects and retains no more information than is necessary for its functions. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Just Three Words changes, if we add new services, or if legal or app-store requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have a privacy question about Just Three Words, contact Bodge Job Apps at support@bodgejobapps.com.
