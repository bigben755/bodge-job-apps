Just Three Words

Three random words. One story.

No imagination? No problem. Just Three Words gives you three random words and one simple challenge: tell a story that connects all three.

It is designed to make bedtime storytelling easier, sillier and a little less repetitive — especially on those evenings when nobody can think what the story should be about.

How it works

Get three words.

Open the app and generate three random words. They might fit together naturally — or they might be completely ridiculous. That is part of the fun.

Your challenge

Connect them in a story.

Make up any story you like, as long as all three words make an appearance. Keep it short, turn it into an epic adventure, or let the children decide where the story goes next.
